# Viral Video Retention Studies — Suggestions

Action items extracted from the retention studies documentation.

---

## OpusClip Short-Form Engagement Analysis (2026)

Deploy automated caption styling models within the editing canvas that auto-select typographic themes based on the detected video genre. Initiate a follow-up micro-study to test the optimal number of words displayed per caption block (e.g., 1-word versus 3-word fragments).

---

## The 3-Second Hook Playbook & Retention Mechanics

Develop a "Hook Scoring Engine" inside the video editor UI. This feature will run a lightweight heuristic check on the first 90 frames of a user's sequence to grade visual velocity, text presence, and acoustic intensity before allowing export.

---

## Audiovisual Pacing & Pattern Interrupt Optimization

Incorporate a "Pacing Heatmap" feature along the editing timeline track. This visual layout will glow amber or red if a talking-head shot remains static for more than 5 seconds without a cut, indicating to the creator that a pattern interrupt is mathematically required.

---

## Psychological Arousal and Transcript Virality Vectors

Train a fine-tuned transformer model specifically on viral video hook templates. Integrate this model into the editor's text-input fields to provide real-time phrase rewriting suggestions that boost estimated emotional arousal percentages prior to voiceover recording.
